## New

- Right-click the empty map to open map and player-view commands, pause the player picture, rotate it by quarter-turns, and lock the player zoom while the GM keeps panning. Arrow keys nudge that locked view. Unfreeze camera brings the GM view back to that picture. Rotate the loaded map by quarter-turns so both views share it. Zooming the GM view while player zoom is locked keeps the player picture where it is.
