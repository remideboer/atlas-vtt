## New

- Right-click the empty map to open map and player-view commands, pause the player picture, rotate it by quarter-turns, and lock the player zoom while the GM keeps panning. Arrow keys nudge that locked view. Unfreeze camera brings the GM view back to that picture. Rotate the loaded map by quarter-turns so both views share it. Zooming the GM view while player zoom is locked keeps the player picture where it is. Freezing the camera keeps the picture already on the player screen. Send map at actual size makes one grid square the chosen physical size on the player screen, and leaves the GM view as it is.
- The fog tool can cover the whole map, so a scene can start fully hidden and then be revealed.

## Fixed

- Unfreezing the player camera moves the GM view onto the picture players are showing, so their picture stays where it is.
- Measured distances show one decimal place, so a 1.5 m cell reads 1.5 m instead of 2 m.
- The fog brush paints and reveals an even stroke while you drag it, where a slow stroke showed a pattern of half-covered dots until it was let go
- The first fog stroke after all fog was deleted shows while you draw it, where it appeared only once let go
